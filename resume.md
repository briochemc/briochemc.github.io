<h1>Interactive CV</h1>
Any CV is just a collection of data, which we typically present in a static Word/PDF document. But I like visuals, and I had made a static visual for it a couple of years back (see <a href="/assets/timeline.svg"> here</a> for the curious), but what I really wanted was an interactive version. I never found the time and energy for it, until now (mostly thanks to AI). I hope this demonstrates how I like to visualise data.
Hover over the timeline snake and the markers (papers and talks/posters) for more details, or click/tap them to keep the details on screen!
